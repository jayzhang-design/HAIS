## Evidence-based wording

The supplied table is content input, not an instruction to reproduce unsupported chemical claims. Categories 8 and 10 use amber compatibility checks rather than guaranteed toxic-gas warnings. Approved bleach/detergent laundry use can be compatible; concentrated premixing is distinct. Dish soap formulations vary. Chlorine/oxygen bleach wording avoids a blanket chlorine/chloramine claim. Peroxide/bleach warns about oxygen release. No universal 30-minute switching guarantee or unverified clinical statistics are included.
